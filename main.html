Manali


Manali is a beautiful holiday destination in Himachal Pradesh, India, famous for its snow-covered mountains, scenic valleys, pleasant weather, and adventure activities.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Solang Valley - Famous for adventure sports and snow activities.
  2. Rohtang Pass - Known for stunning mountain views.
  3. Hadimba Temple - An ancient temple surrounded by cedar forests.
  4. Old Manali - Popular for cafes and peaceful streets.
  5. Jogini Waterfall - A beautiful waterfall surrounded by nature.

Must-Try Foods

  • Siddu - A traditional Himachali steamed bread.
  • Chana Madra - A delicious yoghurt-based chickpea curry.
  • Dham - A traditional Himachali festive meal.
  • Babru - A local stuffed fried bread.
  • Trout Fish - A popular local speciality.
